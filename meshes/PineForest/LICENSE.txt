All models, textures and HDR imagery in this folder come only from that archive:
  - pine-forest-ruins-f35-b10.glb and pine-forest-ruins-f35-b10-casters.glb: the scene's
    models and their textures (trees, saplings, ground cover, rocks, Montaigle ruins, terrain);
  - terrain/: the terrain layer textures forest_leaves_04, forest_ground_04 and rocky_trail,
    and a control mask baked from the scene's terrain material;
  - evening-road-1k.hdr: the scene's evening_road_01_puresky sky HDRI, reduced to 1024 x 512.
