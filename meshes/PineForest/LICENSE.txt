Files
  - pine-forest-ruins-f35-b10.glb: the scene's models and textures (trees, saplings, ground
    cover, rocks, Montaigle ruins, terrain), plus the terrain layer textures forest_leaves_04,
    forest_ground_04 and rocky_trail and a control mask baked from the scene's terrain material
    (embedded images described by asset.extras.pineForest);
  - pine-forest-ruins-f35-b10-casters.glb: simplified shadow-caster models of the same scene.

The demo also uses ../../ibl/evening_road_01_puresky_1k.hdr, the scene's
evening_road_01_puresky sky HDRI from the same archive (CC0 1.0), reduced to 1024 x 512.

Modifications: converted to glTF, cropped around the ruins (35 m in front, 10 m behind), river
removed, materials and geometry simplified, GPU-instanced, vegetation textures downscaled to
512, KTX2/UASTC textures and quantized Meshopt geometry. CC0 requires no attribution; credit to
Poly Haven and its artists is appreciated.
